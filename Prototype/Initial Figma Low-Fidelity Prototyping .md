# Initial Figma Low-Fidelity Prototyping

Initially we worked with the space we were in, considering the circular tables as a key aspect of our designs.

We wanted to create something which worked within this, while also adding a layer which invited all team members to collaborate. This led to us considering a hexagon for our screen shape. 

We liked the idea of increased positive interaction, so including an actual physical figure was a key aspect of making all members feel included and increasing the enjoyment within collaborating.
