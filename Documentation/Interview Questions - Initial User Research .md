# Interview Questions - Initial User Research

1. I want you to think about when you last collaborated on an in-class activity with a team and tell me how it went/what you did. (Follow up: Did you use any physical means to communicate?)
2. How would you define an effective collaborative space for your team? Is there ever a time where you think you have worked in an ideal collaborative space for team work? tell us about it.
3. Tell me about your last in class team-work experience - How did you transfer in-class activities and collaboration to external team work?
4. Think about your last in-class activity - tell me your experience about initially setting up a group collaboration tool? (Why did you pick that tool over others? Did you have a choice?)
5. Walk me through how your group usually decides on their collaboration tools, what kept you engaged and what do you think could have been better (Follow on: what would have kept you more engaged?)
6. Think about a recent team assessment. How did you keep track of what each person contributed or was working on? (Follow up: Was work ever duplicated, missed or the workload felt unfair? How was it handled?)
7. During in-class group work, how do you make sure everyone has a chance to contribute ideas or be involved in decisions? (Follow up: Have you or a team member ever felt left out or less involved? What do you think was the cause?)
